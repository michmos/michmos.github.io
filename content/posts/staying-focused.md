Programming is a lot about keeping your focus on the current problem you are trying to fix. While this can be the most natural thing if you find yourself in perfect flow state, other times it can be very challenging. During my time at 42 I felt overwhelmed many times myself and figured out a couple of techniques that work pretty well in order to not getting distracted


## Introduction
During my time at 42 I heard countless of students talking about being overwhelmed when starting a new project and found myself in the very same situation many times myself. And this is a perfectly normal thing, when you are confronted with a completely new project, without a teacher to take the lead and potentially even with a tech stack you never used - there is just so much you don't know and you have no idea where to start. The problem with feeling overwhelmed is, that it usually doesn't help you to solve the problem;
